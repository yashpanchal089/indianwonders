Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers to common questions about booking with Columbus Travels & Indian Wonders.

What is Columbus Travels' accreditation and background?

Columbus Travels & Services Pvt. Ltd. was established in 1984 (over 40 years ago) in Mumbai, approved by the Department of Tourism, Government of India, and is an accredited Passenger Sales Agent with the International Air Transport Association (IATA). Indian Wonders is our luxury bespoke leisure division established in 1999.

Can any package be 100% customized?

Yes, absolutely. Every tour listed on our portal serves as a proven starting blueprint. You can modify durations, upgrade to Maharaja Palace suites, add private haveli homestays, or link backwater cruises with wildlife safaris. Use our Trip Architect to specify your wishes.

What is unique about your 472-year-old Haveli Homestay package?

Unlike commercial heritage hotels, our Jodhpur haveli homestay is a privately preserved living ancestral mansion belonging to descendants of royal priests. Guests dine on family recipes with the host noble family in the private courtyard, experience Marwari folk musicians, and receive personal access to Jodhpur's history.

How does payment and booking confirmation work?

Once you approve your custom itinerary and formal quotation, bookings are secured with an initial deposit via bank wire transfer, credit card, or authorized merchant gateways. We issue official tax invoices and complete day-by-day travel documentation backed by our IATA credentials.
