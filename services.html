Four Decades of Travel Mastery: The Columbus Legacy

Founded in 1984 by visionary traveler Mrs. Bhavana Agashiwala, Columbus Travels & Services Pvt. Ltd. began with a singular conviction: discerning travelers deserve honest advice, immaculate logistics, and access to the authentic soul of India.

In 1999, Indian Wonders was established as an exclusive bespoke division catering to international dignitaries, scholars, and travelers seeking ancestral haveli stays, private backwater cruises, and high-altitude Himalayan retreats.
